01 — The story

THE RIGHT TOOL
AT THE RIGHT TIME.

When a bicycle breaks and Dad cannot answer, JACK leaves the toolbox and crosses a fantasy world of oversized tools to solve one very real problem.

The problemA loose training-wheel bolt turns an ordinary afternoon into a small emergency.
The callJACK sees the moment no expert is available.
The fixOne precise movement. One secure wheel. Problem solved.
02 — Meet JACK

SMALL TOOL.
BIG HERO
ENERGY.

JACK is the good little devil of the story: small, stubborn and unexpectedly tender. Because of his tiny horn-like shape he looks like a mischievous little creature, but his nature is the opposite. He is “right” in every sense of the word: the right one because he is useful, and the right one because he chooses the good and just action when someone needs help.
