01Brand

Transmissions × TribesA myth before a product.

VoidSignal is not positioned on garments. It is positioned on belonging: a frequency running under the city noise, picked up only by people who live a passion all the way.

Each collection is a Transmission. Each graphic is a mark tuned to one of three tribes — so the customer does not buy a T-shirt, they declare which signal they answer to.

Some people move through the noise and hear something underneath it. A riff that hits before the crowd does. The rhythm of iron at six in the morning. A pulse in the wires at three a.m. They recognise each other on sight. VoidSignal is how.

Three dialects

Graphic languages

The Stage

Gig flyers, torn paper, xerox texture, hand-cut lettering and red electric accents. Raw, layered, loud.

The Iron

An angular VS monogram, tonal on fabric, with speed streaks. Discipline expressed through reduction.

The Grid

Iridescent claw blades over circuit traces, violet-to-cyan chrome. The machine as a second skin.

Campaign casting

Six portraits · one signal

Every tribe is shot twice, with a woman and a man, in its own environment — rock bar, gym floor, neon street. Art-directed with generative image tools, graded on one cold, low-key palette so the whole campaign reads as a single transmission.

03Shop

From signal to cartEvery touchpoint ends in one place.

The official storefront runs on Spreadshop. The work here is the route to it: one conversion path shared by the feed, the website and every single mark, with a drop logic that turns what is not yet live into demand.

  1. Step 01

    Instagram

    Tribe portraits and polls create the first contact; the bio routes to the site.

  2. Step 02

    Website

    The myth and the tribes frame the product before any price appears.

  3. Step 03

    The mark

    Each live mark is deep-linked to its own product page — no catalogue to search.

  4. Step 04

    Shop

    Checkout on the official Spreadshop storefront, opened with a timed launch offer.

Next drop→ waitlist on the site, segmented by tribe → one announcement per drop, sent first to the ones on the frequency.

04Web

voidsignalbrand.comA site you tune into.

The website is the brand's broadcast. It opens on a scroll-scrubbed film — the frequency locks in as you scroll — then tells the myth, hands you the three tribes and lines up the transmission with a direct route to each mark.

Hand-coded, static and fast: no template, no page builder.

voidsignalbrand.com
Signal receivedTorn SignalShop Torn Signal

  • Hero

    Scroll-scrubbed film

    A 121-frame WebP sequence drawn on canvas, with separate desktop and mobile cuts, progressive preloading and a frequency HUD that locks at 87.6 MHz.

  • Story

    Word-by-word myth

    The manifesto lights up one word at a time with scroll position, so the brand story is read, not skimmed.

  • Tribes

    Channel switcher

    A sticky, scroll-driven stage that switches between The Stage, The Iron and The Grid with each tribe's accent and imagery.

  • Commerce

    Direct routes

    Live marks link straight to their product pages; next-drop marks pre-select the tribe in the waitlist form.

  • CRM

    Tribe waitlist

    Sign-up with explicit consent and a privacy notice, posted to a Cloudflare Worker and segmented by tribe for drop announcements.

  • Craft

    Performance & SEO

    Self-hosted variable woff2 fonts, reduced-motion fallbacks, Schema.org Organization, Open Graph cards and sitemap.
