# Home controls regression

Check at 2048×1019, 1024×768 and 390×844.

1. Confirm the gallery is contained between header and footer. Check filters and both circular arrows using the pointer.
2. Spin in both directions using wheel and horizontal drag. Check that ending a drag never activates a card and the document stays fixed.
3. Check that the rear previews progressively soften while the front is sharp. Radial should orbit the chosen card with the stack and show its title and description. Focus should keep the front card sharp and bring side cards through the same plane, with the strongest blur at the edges.
4. Activate the front card again and verify scene or external link opening. Return from a material and verify the active material is featured.
5. Switch filters, including the single-card category, and check navigation and selection.
